# Code of Conduct

CheckMyCode follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people using CheckMyCode. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable anywhere in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions).

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/aliuwahab). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break this code of conduct.
